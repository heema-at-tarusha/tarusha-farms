Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up a home hydroponic garden.

How much electricity does a home hydroponic system consume?

Very little. The submersible magnetic pump is rated at only 35 to 65 Watts and operates on an intermittent cyclic timer (e.g., 15 minutes ON, 45 minutes OFF during sunlight hours). The monthly power consumption is typically less than 15 kWh (approx. ₹100–₹150 per month).

Why do you exclusively use Hot-Dip Galvanized Iron (GI) frames?

Hydroponic systems circulate nutrient-enriched water continuously. In our experience across Delhi NCR, mild steel (even when powder-coated) develops rust within 12–18 months. Hot-dip Galvanized Iron (GI) is rust-immune, structurally rigid, and lasts over 15+ years outdoors.

How effective is the monkey protection enclosure?

Our monkey protection enclosure is constructed with welded heavy-gauge GI wire mesh bolted onto the GI structural frame with a latching entry door. It provides complete physical protection for dripper lines, sensors, and tender crops against monkey troops in areas like Golf Course Extension and DLF Phase 1.

What happens after I receive this indicative estimate?

A TarUsha agronomy engineer will review your dimensions and contact you to conduct a complimentary video site inspection. We confirm electrical point placement, drain gradient, and sun orientation before finalizing your turnkey installation.

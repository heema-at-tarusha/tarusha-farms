About Tarusha

TarUsha is a founder-led urban farming organization passionate about making clean, organic food cultivation accessible inside dense urban spaces. Based in Gurgaon, India, we blend agricultural technology with beautiful aesthetic designs to empower families, corporate hubs, and campuses.

We focus deeply on sustainability (saving up to 90% water compared to soil farming), education (empowering students with STEM botanic courses), and community impact by localizing crops and mitigating food transit carbon footprints.

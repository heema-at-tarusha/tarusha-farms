A New Dimension

A New Dimension to Interior Design

As interior spaces evolve to incorporate wellness, nature and functionality, hydroponics presents an opportunity to introduce living, growing systems into thoughtfully designed environments.

From functional kitchen gardens to integrated green installations, these systems can become an additional design consideration for residential and commercial projects.

Residential Living

Kitchens, Dining Nooks & Urban Balcony Transitions

Explore the integration of compact hydroponic herb gardens and edible green systems into kitchens, balconies, dining areas and contemporary residential spaces.

  • Seamless joinery and under-counter or island cabinetry integration
  • Clean recirculating water delivery with zero soil, mud, or pest attraction

Design Considerations

More Than Decorative Greenery

Hydroponics introduces the possibility of functional growing systems into designed environments. Key technical and spatial factors we explore together:

🥗

Functional Edible Gardens

Moving beyond purely ornamental foliage by integrating culinary herbs, salad greens, and microgreens that offer real harvest utility right where food or drinks are prepared.

📐

Cabinetry & Architectural Integration

Designing system footprints to sit flush within custom millwork, credenzas, recessed wall alcoves, or bespoke structural room dividers without clumsy exterior plumbing.

🌿

Compact & Space-Efficient Systems

Utilizing vertical geometry and low-profile channel depths to maximize living plant density while respecting valuable interior floor space and clear circulation paths.

💡

Lighting & Growing Environment

Balancing Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) spectrums with ambient color temperatures, ensuring plants thrive while maintaining warm, comfortable interior visual moods.

💧

Water Management & System Accessibility

Engineering closed-loop, leak-proof recirculating systems with concealed catchment, simple manual or plumbed refill access, and fail-safe sensors.

🛠️

Maintenance & Operational Planning

Designing straightforward nutrient replenishment routines, accessible clean-out points, and practical operational guidelines so end-users or service teams can maintain vigor effortlessly.

Common questions from interior designers and architects exploring hydroponics integration.

Requirements depend on system scale:

  • Compact / Cabinet Systems: Standard 230V electrical point for timed LED lighting and a small low-wattage circulation pump. Water replenishment can be handled manually via a concealed, slide-out reservoir without requiring continuous plumbing.
  • Larger Living Wall Installations: A dedicated 230V power supply, nearby fresh water line with a shutoff valve for auto-top-up, and a gravity drain or floor waste connection for scheduled nutrient flushes.

We work directly with your MEP contractors to provide exact electrical load and plumbing connection drawings during the design development phase.

Traditional pink/purple agricultural grow lights are visually unsuitable for refined living spaces. We specify calibrated warm-white full-spectrum LED fixtures (3000K–4000K with high CRI > 90) that deliver the required Photosynthetically Active Radiation (PAR) while seamlessly blending with your interior lighting plan.

Fixtures can be recessed into millwork pelmets, integrated into ceiling tracks, or mounted as architectural accent bars on digital schedules (typically 12–14 hours daily).

Space Discovery & Vision

Sunlight requirements depend entirely on what you wish to grow:

  • Fruiting vegetables (Tomatoes, Bell Peppers): 5 to 6+ hours of direct sunlight.
  • Leafy greens & herbs (Lettuce, Mint, Spinach): 3 to 4 hours of gentle or filtered sun.
  • Ornamental & Air-purifying foliage (Ferns, Pothos, Syngonium): Bright indirect light without direct scorch.

During our space assessment, we map your sun angles to recommend the ideal botanical selection.

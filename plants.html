Living Wall Principles

Key Factors in Hydroponic Wall Success

A flourishing vertical garden depends on matching plant biology with automated hydroponics.

☀️

Light Gradients & Placement

Vertical walls naturally experience lighting variances. Position light-demanding species like Mint, Tradescantia, and Ivy at the top or near grow fixtures, placing shade lovers like Peace Lilies and Aglaonemas near the lower modules.

💧

Automated Drip & Drainage

Hydroponic living walls use closed-loop circulation with expanded clay, perlite, or felt pockets. Program irrigation timers for gentle micro-drip cycles that keep roots consistently oxygenated without waterlogging.

🧪

Nutrient EC & pH Balance

For lush vegetative foliage, maintain a steady pH between 5.5 and 6.5 with an Electrical Conductivity (EC) of 1.2 to 1.8 mS/cm. Mild, balanced nutrition ensures vigorous foliage without salt buildup.

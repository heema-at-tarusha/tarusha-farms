==============================================================================
TarUsha - Commercial Hydroponic Farm Photography & Media Assets
==============================================================================

Place your high-resolution commercial farm photographs into the corresponding
folders:

1. assets/commercial-farms/projects/
   Store photographs of commercial polyhouse facilities, greenhouse structures,
   NFT growing lines, Dutch bucket vine crop rows, automated fertigation rooms,
   nursery benches, and completed facilities.
   Recommended naming:
   - farm1-main.jpg
   - farm1-greenhouse.jpg
   - farm1-nft-channels.jpg
   - farm1-fertigation.jpg
   - farm2-main.jpg
   - farm2-dutch-buckets.jpg

2. assets/commercial-farms/capabilities/
   Store photographs representing specific engineering capabilities:
   - planning-layout.jpg
   - greenhouse-polyhouse.jpg
   - nft-commercial.jpg
   - substrate-dutch-bucket.jpg
   - fertigation-dosing.jpg
   - nursery-seedling.jpg
   - climate-automation.jpg
   - commissioning.jpg

Fallback behavior:
If a custom image is not yet provided, the application gracefully displays
verified assets from assets/farm.jpg, assets/turnkey.jpeg, and assets/indoor_dutch_bucket.jpeg.
==============================================================================
